About

I'm a researcher working on projects in Coastal Engineering, Earth Observation, and Machine Learning. I use large-scale satellite imagery, geospatial data, wave and climate datasets, and cloud-based tools to monitor and analyse coastal and deltaic dynamics. I also have experience developing low-cost instruments for monitoring water quality and physical oceanographic variables, and participate and design in coastal oceanographic field campaigns involving in-situ measurements of hydrodynamic and morphological processes.

Research

Our work investigates how tides, waves, and sea-level variability influence shoreline change, barrier island behavior, and deltaic environments. This supports coastal hazard assessment and long-term adaptation strategies.
