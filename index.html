About

I'm a researcher working on projects in Coastal Engineering, Earth Observation, and Machine Learning. I use large-scale satellite imagery, geospatial data, wave and climate datasets, and cloud-based tools to monitor and analyse coastal and deltaic dynamics.

I also have experience developing low-cost instruments for monitoring water quality and physical oceanographic variables, and participate and design in coastal oceanographic field campaigns involving in-situ measurements of hydrodynamic and morphological processes.
